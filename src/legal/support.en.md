## Get in touch

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to [hola@luffly.app](mailto:hola@luffly.app?subject=Luffly). For a bug, please tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, Luffly version and what you were doing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an account?

No. Luffly has no sign-up or login. Your data lives on your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud.

### How does the season work?

The Season tab splits the year into three phases: before launch, the season and haul-out, and shows what’s due in the current one. By default it follows a Mediterranean calendar, launching in May and hauling out in October. If your boat goes in or comes out on other dates, change them in Settings › Your season. From December onwards, the season shown is the next year’s.

### What are the seven systems?

Standing rigging, running rigging, sails, hull & underwater, deck, auxiliary engine and safety. When you add your sailboat, Luffly creates the usual elements of each system for your length and engine type. You can add your own, change any interval or delete what doesn’t apply to your boat.

### Where do the intervals come from?

They are starting points from manufacturers’ manuals, riggers and sailmakers, marked as such in the app. Follow your own manual or your professional’s advice, and change the intervals to match.

### How do reminders work?

For each element with a date, Luffly sends a reminder some days before it’s due and another on the day, at 9:00. Turn them on in Settings › Notifications. Elements that go by engine hours can’t be predicted to the day, so they show in the Season tab: keep the engine hours up to date there.

### Why does an element say “No data”?

Luffly needs to know when it was last done. Log the last intervention, or set a starting point (date or engine hours) without adding anything to the history.

### What does the free plan include?

The season and all seven systems, reminders for 15 elements plus the ITB and the insurance, the full history, the checklists and iCloud sync, for one boat. The 15 are chosen by importance across the whole boat. The rest stay visible with a PRO badge and no reminders; nothing is hidden or deleted. To change which 15 have reminders, turn off “Active” on one element and turn it on for another.

### What does Luffly Pro add?

Reminders for every element, the sail inventory and rigging age advice, costs by system with an estimate of the next 12 months, papers on board and every expiry date, regulations and safety equipment by zone, cruising abroad, spares on board, the job list for the yard, the PDF logbook and CSV export, and unlimited boats and photos. It’s sold as a yearly subscription with a 14-day free trial or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

### Why don’t I see the free trial?

Apple gives the free trial once per Apple Account. If you have already had it, Luffly offers Pro without a trial.

### How do I sync between my devices?

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on each device. Luffly syncs through your private iCloud on its own; it can take a moment after you first open the app.

### I switched iPhones and don’t see my Pro purchase.

Go to Settings › Luffly Pro › Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

In your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Luffly. Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. To request a refund, go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### How do I export my history?

With Pro, use the Export menu in the History tab: a PDF logbook to share with a buyer, your insurer or your mechanic, or a CSV file with every intervention for a spreadsheet.

### Does it cover the rules where I sail?

Luffly’s regulations follow the Spanish rules (ITB, navigation zones and equipment), and the cruising abroad section summarises what France, Italy, Greece, Croatia, Malta, Portugal, Turkey and Montenegro add. It’s a guide: always check the regulations in force.

### How do I delete my data?

To delete a boat with everything in it, go to Settings › Boats and swipe left on it; it’s deleted on all your devices. To delete everything, delete the app and, if iCloud was on, also delete Luffly’s data in your iPhone’s Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.
