> **In short:** Luffly doesn't collect, see or sell your data. Your boats, maintenance records and attachments stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account. There are no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Who is responsible

Luffly is developed by Pedro Serra Comas (Spain), who is the controller for any data mentioned in this policy. Contact: [hola@luffly.app](mailto:hola@luffly.app).

## Data you enter

Boats, engines, maintenance elements, service records, checklists, notes, photos and scanned documents are stored **on your device**. If iCloud is enabled on your device, Luffly syncs them through **your private iCloud database** (Apple CloudKit), so they appear on your other devices. That database belongs to your Apple Account: the developer has no access to it. Apple handles it under its own [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are only used when you choose to scan an invoice or a document, or attach a photo. Images are saved with your records, as described above, and are never uploaded to the developer.

## Notifications

Maintenance reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No data is sent to any server to deliver them.

## Purchases

Luffly Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The app checks whether you have Luffly Pro directly with Apple on your device (StoreKit). We don't receive your purchase details or your Apple Account, and no third-party service is involved.

## What Luffly does not do

- No user accounts or sign-in.
- No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
- No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties.
- No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

## This website

luffly.app does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other websites. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser) for a short time for security and to keep the site running.

## Export and deletion

With Luffly Pro you can export your history as a PDF logbook or a CSV file. Deleting a boat or a record in the app also deletes it from iCloud; to delete a boat with everything in it, go to Settings › Boats and swipe left on it. Uninstalling the app removes the copy on your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to your iPhone’s Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Luffly.

## Your rights

Because the developer does not hold your data, you control it directly from your device. If you are in the European Union you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection under the GDPR, and you can write to the contact above with any question. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency ([aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)) or your local authority.

## Children

Luffly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
