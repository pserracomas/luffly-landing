## Luffly and Luffly Pro

Luffly is free to download and use with one boat. The free plan covers the season and all seven systems, keeps reminders on for 15 elements plus the ITB and the insurance, and includes the full history, the checklists and iCloud sync. Luffly Pro unlocks the rest (described on the [home page](/#pro) and in the App Store) and is available as:

- **Yearly subscription**, with a 14-day free trial for new subscribers.
- **Lifetime purchase**, a single one-time payment.

Prices are shown in the App Store in your local currency before you confirm. If Pro ends, nothing you entered is deleted: elements beyond the free plan’s 15 stay in the app without reminders, and you can choose which 15 keep them.

- Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or at the end of the free trial if you don’t cancel before then.
